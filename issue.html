ISSUE: INCREASING ELECTRICITY DEMAND & SEDENTARY LIFESTYLE

Electricity demand

The demand for electricity is skyrocketing with all the new technology coming out like Artificial intelligence which heavily relies on powerful CPUs and GPUs which takes a lot of electricity to run; as well as the growing population of humans the demand will keep growing.

Sedentary lifestyle

People today are becoming more sedentary than ever, spending hours sitting at desks, scrolling through their phones, or streaming shows instead of being physically active; with modern conveniences and digital entertainment taking over daily life, this growing trend is leading to poorer physical health and fitness overall.
